# Decision templates

Copy the appropriate template to a dated ADR before implementing a major boundary change. A filled template is a proposal until its named approvers sign it and its evidence links resolve. These templates do not authorize or enable any feature.

- [Game-facing input](game-input.md)
- [Updater infrastructure](updater.md)
- [Code signing](signing.md)
- [Data migration](migration.md)

Every decision records owner, context, alternatives, misuse/failure risks, privacy/security implications, acceptance gates, rollback, and approval. Do not replace measured results with an assertion of compliance.
